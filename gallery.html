Lebron 1

Year Released: 2003

The Nike LeBron 1 marked the inception of LeBron James’ signature line. Before LeBron was drafted as the No. 1 overall pick in the 2003 NBA Draft, Nike signed a groundbreaking 7-year, fully guaranteed sneaker contract with him, worth an astounding $87 million. Despite initial skepticism, LeBron exceeded all expectations, making the executives at Nike look like geniuses. The LeBron 1 featured both Nike Air Zoom technology and Nike Air technology, cutting-edge cushioning systems at the time. Produced in seven colorways, it introduced King James and his signature sneaker to the league.
